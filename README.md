# Cutest Picture — make.fun entry (cutest-picture-01)

A fluffy grey-white kitten with huge green eyes and an open-mouth
smile, wearing a cosy pink knitted scarf, against a flat pink
background. Generated by the entrant with Stable Diffusion XL on
their own PC and saved as a 1024×1024 JPEG.

Round 2 replaces the round-1 tea party: the tea party scored 75.73%
in the dev heats, and the judge's own notes said its four animals and
many props "compete for attention and read weakly at small size" —
"at 300px the subject becomes a crowd rather than one clear cute
thing". Every one of its five losses was against a single focused
subject with one story-touch. This entry answers that directly: one
kitten, one scarf, one flat background, readable at a glance.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the picture held in
this repository (`entry.jpg`, 1024×1024 JPEG, 161,116 bytes) to
`/output/entry.png`. The program is deterministic: running it twice writes
byte-identical output.

## Run locally

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
